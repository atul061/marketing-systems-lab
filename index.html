The 6 tags in plain words

utm_idWhich big promotion?One course batch · gsf28_b01
utm_campaignWhich activity?{utm_id}_{activity}_{nn}_{variant}
utm_contentWhich exact creative?{type}_{what}_{nn} · reel_topper_01
utm_sourceWhere was it shared?Title Case, exact spelling · Instagram
utm_mediumHow was it shared?One of 11 · organic_video
utm_termWhich keyword or ad set?Paid ads only
  • Sources are Title Case: Instagram, not instagram. The CRM matches the exact spelling.
  • Everything else is lowercase_with_underscores, no spaces.
  • The platform goes in source, never in medium (messaging, not whatsapp).
  • One creative = one utm_content. New poster? New number.
  • Never tag links from one levelupias.com page to another.
Read them as one sentence: For utm_id, during utm_campaign, we posted utm_content on utm_source as utm_medium.
